Fattoush Salad

Crispy fried flatbread, summer vegetables and a pomegranate molasses dressing — bright, textural and utterly addictive. Make the dressing first; everything else takes minutes.

Prep15 mins
Cook5 mins
Serves4
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

Salad

  • 2 large flatbreads or pitta
  • 4 tbsp olive oil (for frying)
  • 4 ripe tomatoes, roughly chopped
  • 1 cucumber, halved, deseeded, sliced
  • 4 radishes, thinly sliced
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • Large handful fresh mint leaves
  • Large handful flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
  • ½ tsp sumac, plus more to finish

Dressing

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp pomegranate molasses
  • 1 small garlic clove, crushed
  • ½ tsp sumac
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Fry the flatbread. Tear the flatbread into rough 3cm pieces. Heat olive oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Fry the bread pieces in batches, turning, until golden and crisp all over. Drain on kitchen paper and season with a little salt and sumac while still hot.
  2. Make the dressing. Whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, pomegranate molasses, garlic and sumac. Season generously with salt. Taste — it should be tangy and slightly sweet.
  3. Assemble. Combine the tomatoes, cucumber, radishes, spring onions, mint and parsley in a large bowl. Pour over most of the dressing and toss gently.
  4. Add the bread at the last moment. Scatter the crispy flatbread over the salad just before serving. Drizzle remaining dressing over the top, dust with sumac, and serve immediately.

Chef's Notes

The bread must go in last — the second it hits the dressed salad it starts to soften. For extra crunch, bake the torn flatbread in the oven at 200°C for 8 minutes instead of frying.

Pomegranate molasses is the non-negotiable ingredient here. It can be found in any Middle Eastern grocer and most large supermarkets.
