Baked Kibbeh (Kibbeh bil Sanieh)

A layered bake of spiced minced lamb and bulgur wheat — crisp and golden on top, tender and fragrant throughout. One of the most celebrated dishes in Lebanese home cooking.

Prep40 mins
Cook50 mins
Serves6
DifficultyIntermediate

Ingredients

Kibbeh shell

  • 500g lean lamb mince
  • 250g fine bulgur wheat, rinsed
  • 1 medium onion, grated
  • 1½ tsp ground allspice
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • 1½ tsp fine sea salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Filling

  • 400g lamb mince
  • 2 onions, finely diced
  • 80g pine nuts
  • 1 tsp allspice
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Soak the bulgur. Cover the rinsed bulgur with cold water and soak for 20 minutes. Drain thoroughly, pressing out excess moisture with your hands.
  2. Make the shell mixture. Combine the soaked bulgur, 500g lamb mince, grated onion, spices and salt. Knead with damp hands for 3–4 minutes until the mixture is smooth and cohesive. Keep a bowl of cold water nearby to wet your hands as you work.
  3. Cook the filling. Fry the diced onions in olive oil until soft and golden, about 10 minutes. Add the pine nuts and toast until golden. Add 400g lamb mince and cook, breaking it up, until browned. Season with allspice, cinnamon, salt and pepper. Set aside to cool slightly.
  4. Assemble. Preheat the oven to 190°C/fan 170°C. Oil a 30×20cm baking dish. Press half the kibbeh shell mixture into the dish in an even layer (dip your hands in water — it helps). Spread the filling evenly over the top, then carefully press the remaining shell mixture over the filling to form a top layer.
  5. Score and bake. With a sharp knife, score the top in a diamond pattern. Drizzle generously with olive oil. Bake for 45–50 minutes until deep golden and crisp on top.
  6. Rest and serve. Leave to rest for 10 minutes before cutting. Serve with plain yoghurt, a green salad and flatbread.

Chef's Notes

The key to the shell is moisture control — too wet and it won't hold, too dry and it cracks. If the mixture is too stiff, add a tablespoon of cold water; if too soft, add a little more bulgur.

Leftovers reheat brilliantly in the oven at 160°C for 15 minutes, or in a dry frying pan to re-crisp the base.
