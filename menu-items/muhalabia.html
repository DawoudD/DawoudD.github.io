Muhalabia

A Lebanese milk pudding — the Levant's answer to panna cotta. Silky, faintly floral, and impossibly light. Set gently with cornflour and scented with rose water, then topped with crushed pistachios and a thread of honey.

CourseDessert
DietaryVegetarian, GF
PortionIndividual

The Dish

Whole milk and double cream are heated gently with sugar and a generous pour of rose water. Cornflour is whisked in to set the pudding — just enough to hold its shape when unmoulded, with a trembling, barely-there texture.

Set overnight in individual glasses or cups. Served at the table with a spoonful of crushed pistachios, a drizzle of raw honey and a scattering of dried rose petals. It is a quiet, beautiful dessert — understated enough to follow anything.

Allergens

Dairy (milk, cream) Tree nuts (pistachios)

Can be made with oat milk for a dairy-free version — please advise at time of booking.

Key Ingredients

  • Base Whole milk, double cream
  • Set Cornflour
  • Flavour Rose water, caster sugar
  • Garnish Crushed pistachios, raw honey, dried rose petals

Chef's Note

Muhalabia is the dessert I reach for when I want to offer something that feels refined without being heavy. After a six-course meal, it lands perfectly — cool, fragrant and just sweet enough.

The amount of rose water is a matter of taste. I lean generous — but this is one of those ingredients where a millilitre too much tips it into soap. Balance is everything.
