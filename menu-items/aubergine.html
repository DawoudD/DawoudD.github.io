Charred Aubergine & Tahini

A whole aubergine placed directly over an open flame until the skin turns to carbon and the inside becomes smoky, sweet and collapsed. The flesh is scooped, dressed and finished at the table.

CourseMain
DietaryVegan, GF
PortionIndividual

The Dish

The aubergine is charred whole on a gas flame for 20–25 minutes, turning regularly until entirely blackened. The smoke penetrates through the skin into the flesh, giving an intensity that no oven can replicate.

The skin is peeled away, the flesh is laid open on the plate and dressed with tahini thinned with lemon and garlic, a spoonful of pomegranate seeds, toasted pine nuts and fresh flat-leaf parsley. Finished with olive oil and za'atar.

Allergens

Sesame (tahini) Tree nuts (pine nuts)

Key Ingredients

  • Main Whole aubergine, flame-charred
  • Sauce Tahini, lemon, garlic, cold water
  • Garnish Pomegranate seeds, pine nuts, flat-leaf parsley
  • Finish Cold-pressed olive oil, za'atar

Chef's Note

This is a dish where the cooking technique IS the flavour. The open-flame char is non-negotiable — it is what separates a smoky, complex plate from a straightforwardly roasted vegetable.

It is also one of the best plant-based dishes I know for satisfying guests who aren't expecting to be impressed by a vegetable.
