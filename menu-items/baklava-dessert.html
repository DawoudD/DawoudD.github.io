Pistachio Baklava

Forty layers of butter-brushed filo, finely crushed pistachios, and a rose-water and orange blossom syrup. Served with a small cup of cardamom coffee — the traditional pairing that cuts the sweetness perfectly.

CourseDessert / Petit four
DietaryVegan
Portion2–3 pieces per person

The Dish

Made fresh the morning of service — never in advance. Each tray has forty layers of filo, each one brushed by hand with clarified butter before being stacked. The pistachio filling is lightly sweetened and mixed with cinnamon before being spread in a single even layer between the two halves.

Scored into diamonds before baking, drenched in cold syrup the moment it leaves the oven. Plated individually with crushed pistachios and served alongside a small cardamom coffee.

Allergens

Gluten (filo) Tree nuts (pistachios)

Dairy-free if made with vegan butter — available on request.

Key Ingredients

  • Pastry Filo, brushed with clarified butter
  • Filling Crushed pistachios, caster sugar, cinnamon
  • Syrup Sugar, rose water, orange blossom water, lemon
  • Pairing Cardamom coffee

Chef's Note

The cut must be made before the tray goes in the oven — a sharp knife through cold, layered filo glides cleanly. After baking, the pastry shatters unpredictably. Don't skip the pre-bake cut.
