Fattoush & Pomegranate

A salad built on contrast — the crunch of fried flatbread against soft summer tomatoes, and a dressing that balances sharp pomegranate molasses with fruity olive oil. Finished with sumac and fresh herbs.

CourseStarter
DietaryVegan
PortionIndividual

The Dish

Village bread is torn and fried in olive oil until shatteringly crisp, then scattered over a bed of vine tomatoes, cucumber, radish and spring onion. Dressed moments before serving with pomegranate molasses, lemon and a generous hand of sumac.

Fresh mint and flat-leaf parsley bring brightness. The bread catches the dressing and softens just slightly at the edges — the whole thing is assembled and plated at the very last second.

Allergens

Gluten (flatbread)

Can be served without flatbread for a fully gluten-free version.

Key Ingredients

  • Bread Village flatbread, fried in olive oil
  • Leaves Vine tomatoes, cucumber, radish
  • Herbs Mint, flat-leaf parsley
  • Dressing Pomegranate molasses, lemon, olive oil
  • Spice Sumac

Chef's Note

Fattoush is almost always underestimated. At its best it is one of the most satisfying salads in the world — bright, textural, and genuinely difficult to stop eating.
