Slow-Roast Shoulder of Lamb

Seven hours at low heat, sealed tightly in foil with cumin, coriander, preserved lemon and garlic. The lamb doesn't so much roast as surrender. Shredded at the table. Silence follows.

CourseMain
DietaryGF, contains lamb
PortionSharing centrepiece

The Dish

A whole bone-in shoulder, studded with garlic and preserved lemon, rubbed with a spice paste of cumin, coriander, cinnamon and smoked paprika. Sealed tightly in foil with a splash of stock and roasted at 160°C for the better part of a day.

The result is meat that pulls apart with two forks, surrounded by its own concentrated jus. Brought to the table whole and shredded in front of guests. Served with flatbread, yoghurt, a herb salad and jewelled rice.

Allergens

None (main dish)

Flatbread accompaniment contains gluten. Rice and yoghurt accompaniments are GF.

Key Ingredients

  • Protein Bone-in lamb shoulder, sourced from a named farm
  • Aromatics Garlic, preserved lemon
  • Spice Cumin, coriander, cinnamon, smoked paprika, cayenne
  • Liquid Light lamb stock, lemon juice
  • Sides Jewelled rice, yoghurt, herb salad, flatbread

Chef's Note

The discipline of this dish is patience. Open the foil too early and you lose the steam that makes the meat collapse. The reward for restraint is a jus that needs nothing more than a ladle and a bowl.
