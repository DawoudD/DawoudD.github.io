Hummus bi Tahini

The first dish on every table I set. Made from dried chickpeas soaked overnight and blended with an abundance of quality tahini, lemon and garlic. Served with warm hand-pulled flatbread and cold-pressed olive oil.

CourseStarter
DietaryVegan, GF option
PortionSharing

The Dish

This is not hummus from a tub. It begins with dried chickpeas, soaked overnight, then cooked until they dissolve between your fingers. Blended still warm with tahini, lemon and a single clove of garlic, the result is pale, silky and intensely flavoured.

Served in a wide bowl with a pool of cold-pressed olive oil, a pinch of sumac and warm flatbread baked to order.

Allergens

Sesame Gluten (flatbread)

GF flatbread alternative available on request. Always inform your chef of any allergies when booking.

Key Ingredients

  • Base Dried chickpeas, soaked 12 hours
  • Tahini Al Nakhil stone-ground sesame paste
  • Acid Freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • Allium Single clove of raw garlic
  • Finish Cold-pressed olive oil, sumac, paprika
  • Bread Warm flatbread, baked to order

Chef's Note

The ice-cold water added at the end of blending is what turns this from beige paste into something silky and pale. It's a small detail that makes an enormous difference.
