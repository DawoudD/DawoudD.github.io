Dessert · Vegetarian

Knafeh

Shredded pastry baked over warm stretchy cheese, flipped and soaked with cold orange blossom syrup, finished with crushed pistachios. A dessert that stops conversation. Eaten immediately, while the cheese is still molten.

CourseDessert
DietaryVegetarian
PortionSharing or individual

The Dish

Kataifi pastry — thin strands of shredded filo — is mixed with clarified butter and pressed into a pan over a layer of akkawi cheese and ricotta. Baked until the pastry is deep golden and the cheese beneath is warm and elastic.

The pan is inverted, revealing the burnished crust, and immediately doused with cold orange blossom and rose water syrup. The sizzle and contrast of hot pastry and cold syrup is part of the theatre. Finished with pistachios and served within two minutes of leaving the oven.

Allergens

Gluten (pastry) Dairy (cheese) Tree nuts (pistachios)

Key Ingredients

  • Pastry Kataifi (shredded filo), clarified butter
  • Cheese Akkawi, ricotta, semolina
  • Syrup Sugar, orange blossom water, rose water, lemon
  • Finish Crushed pistachios

Chef's Note

The syrup must always be cold when it meets the hot pastry. This is the rule — not a suggestion. Hot syrup produces a soggy result. Cold syrup hits the crust and is absorbed instantly, leaving the pastry crisp.
