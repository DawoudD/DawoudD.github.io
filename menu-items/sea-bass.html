Chermoula Sea Bass

Whole sea bass baked over a bed of fennel, tomato and white wine, finished at the last moment with a vibrant North African chermoula of coriander, cumin, preserved lemon and chilli. Light, fragrant and unmistakably summer.

CourseMain
DietaryGF, contains fish
PortionIndividual or sharing

The Dish

A whole line-caught sea bass, scored deeply and packed with sliced lemon and fresh herbs, laid over a tray of shaved fennel, cherry tomatoes and a splash of dry white wine. Roasted until the skin blisters and crisps.

Chermoula — a North African herb and spice paste of flat-leaf coriander, cumin, garlic, preserved lemon and chilli — is spooned over the fish at the table, the heat from the fish releasing its fragrance. Served with couscous and a green salad.

Allergens

Fish Sulphites (wine)

Couscous contains gluten. Can be substituted with rice on request.

Key Ingredients

  • Fish Whole line-caught sea bass
  • Base Fennel, cherry tomatoes, white wine
  • Chermoula Coriander, cumin, garlic, preserved lemon, chilli, olive oil
  • Sides Herbed couscous, green salad

Chef's Note

Chermoula improves with time — I make it 24 hours ahead and let the flavours deepen. It also works beautifully as a marinade for chicken or vegetables.
