Fried Kibbeh

Torpedo-shaped croquettes of finely ground lamb mixed into a shell of spiced bulgur wheat. Fried until the outside is shatteringly crisp and the inside remains tender, fragrant and impossibly juicy.

CourseStarter
DietaryContains lamb
Portion3 per person

The Dish

Kibbeh is the national dish of Lebanon and one of the most technically demanding things I make. The shell is formed from lean lamb mince worked together with fine bulgur, onion, allspice and cinnamon until smooth enough to be shaped around a filling of sautéed minced lamb and toasted pine nuts.

Each piece is shaped by hand into the classic football shape, then deep-fried to order. Served with labneh (strained yoghurt), fresh mint and a wedge of lemon.

Allergens

Gluten (bulgur wheat) Dairy (labneh) Tree nuts (pine nuts)

Key Ingredients

  • Shell Fine bulgur wheat, lean lamb mince
  • Spice Allspice, cinnamon, black pepper
  • Filling Spiced lamb, caramelised onion, pine nuts
  • Accompaniment Labneh, fresh mint, lemon

Chef's Note

The shell must be mixed with ice-cold water and shaped with wet hands — warmth is the enemy of a tight kibbeh. Every piece is shaped individually, by feel, which is why no two look identical.
